# Research: making BERT a general app

Researched 2026-09-08. Application source inspected: `30007889bd9948142090b8afdf692a9e4ab5626e`; starting repository HEAD: `df97353`. This supplements [the critique](CRITIQUE-2026-09-08.md). These are product recommendations, not accepted implementation commitments.

## Judgment

**BERT needs an activity people value, a reason to return, and a recognizable character. More utility panels will not supply those on their own.** The first restructuring improved access to features, but its priorities still reflect the original widget app:

- **Two of four navigation destinations are financial tools.** `MainActivity.kt:34–35` defines Home, Market, Holdings and Studio. That is a count of destinations, not a measurement of screen area. For a general BERT app, games and creativity deserve comparable visibility.
- **Home introduces the ecosystem without delivering much of it.** `HomeScreen.kt:24–56` contains a fixed welcome, market summary, tool links and three website roots. Someone looking for Flappy Bert or drawing must discover the right website and navigate again. There is no BERT dispatch, event or playable activity here.
- **“Studio” describes different products.** Native `StudioScreen.kt:41` is theme configuration; the [web Studio](https://berthalla.io/studio/) is an image generator. A shared Create destination with clear Art and Personalize sections would make that distinction understandable.

These are source and content findings. Native appearance, touch behavior and visual quality remain **UNVERIFIED**; this research adds no screenshot evidence or numerical visual score.

## What BERT already has

Public pages, their linked scripts and selected public GET endpoints were inspected. [Timestamped observations](evidence/2026-09-08/ecosystem-research.json) record response hashes and selected fields. HTTP 200 establishes retrieval, not a completed user journey, mobile compatibility or sustained reliability.

| Experience | Evidence available now | Sensible app treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bert dispatch and mood | [Public status JSON](https://berthalla.io/status.json) exposes `updated_at`, `mood` and `latest_post`. It has no per-post ID, publication date, permalink or archive in the observed response. | A small latest-dispatch card with source refresh age. A chronological news feed needs a richer contract. |
| Flappy Bert | The same endpoint supplied an autumn event marked live, a December 1, 2026 end date, five leaderboard rows and a Telegram play URL. [Playground](https://www.bert.global/playground) links the same bot. | Current event details plus “Play in Telegram.” Scores, payouts and gameplay were not independently validated. |
| Draw Bert | [Web Studio](https://berthalla.io/studio/) advertises three daily draws; its [status endpoint](https://berthalla.io/studio/api/status) returned `remaining: 3`, `closed: false`. Playground also links a distinct [Telegram DrawBert bot](https://t.me/DrawBert_bot). | Open web creation first. Generation, quota equivalence between channels, and native API support remain unverified; no generation request was made. |
| Music | [Bert Music](https://berthalla.io/music/) contains three track links and a Foundation YouTube channel. | A compact collection opening the canonical videos. A native streaming player adds little at this catalog size. Playback was not tested. |
| Daily puzzles | [Berthalla](https://berthalla.io/) advertises Where’s Bert? and Odd Bert Out through its character account. | Link to the existing activity initially. Native play needs licensed scenes, answers, scheduling and a maintained publishing source. |
| Woofhub | [Woofhub](https://woofhub.com/) presents dog profiles, adoption and care features, with its own account flows. | Useful entry points under Explore. No authenticated journey or supported integration/SSO contract was established. |
| Farmhalla / Hold The Line | [Official Playground](https://www.bert.global/playground) labels both coming soon. | Keep out of the playable catalog until their owners confirm launch readiness and mobile QA passes. |

An important data trap: the [Berthalla homepage](https://berthalla.io/) returned summer-event fallback HTML, while the fetched JSON described the autumn event. Its [public script](https://berthalla.io/app.js?v=8), lines 208–211, requests that JSON and retains fallback content on failure. Do not scrape the fallback into native cards. Use a validated data contract, distinguish cache age from event status, and expire events using `ends_at`. The endpoint is a promising source, not yet a documented mobile API. Keep financial quotes on the existing mint-validated quote service.

The [official BERT site](https://www.bert.global/) connects the character to community and helping dogs. That supports an occasional sourced shelter story or community spotlight. It does not establish a verified impact database or justify invented donation counters.

## Three useful product references

These are pattern comparisons from first-party product documentation, not hands-on competitor reviews or evidence of retention uplift.

| Reference | Observed pattern | Application to BERT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Nintendo Today!](https://www.nintendo.com/us/mobile-apps/nintendo-today/) | Personalized updates, event calendar, character themes, content/calendar widgets and matching wallpapers. | Closest structural reference: new activity and character identity form the app; widgets extend it onto the phone. Start with a publishing cadence BERT can sustain rather than promising daily news. |
| [LEGO Play](https://www.lego.com/en-us/service/help-topics/article/lego-play-app?age-gate=grown_up) | Creation tools, minigames, videos and avatar customization; shared content has an explicit moderation operation. | Give fans something to make and share. Local exports to existing communities are a smaller first step than operating a new social feed. Its child-focused audience is not evidence about BERT’s audience. |
| [Finch adventures](https://help.finchcare.com/hc/en-us/articles/37779979512845-Going-on-an-Adventure) | An activity leads to a pet adventure, then a short story and a response that develops the character. | A possible later BERT interaction: a brief walk or municipal errand ending in a character moment. Emotional attachment is a hypothesis to test; this is not a proposal to copy Finch’s self-care product. |

## Recommended structure

Prototype **Home / Explore / Create / Tools**. Put settings in a consistent toolbar action. Avoid adding an account-shaped “Profile” destination before it has a purpose.

| Destination | Main job | First useful contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | What is happening, and what can I do? | Bert’s latest dispatch and mood, one featured activity, current event when valid, quick return to a favorite. Market summary can be an optional small card. |
| Explore | Find BERT experiences | Flappy Bert, music, community and Woofhub links, with clear browser/Telegram labels and availability. No search needed for this small catalog. |
| Create | Make and keep BERT things | Draw Bert entry, wallpapers, widget previews and setup. Separate Art from Personalize; saved exports can follow. |
| Tools | Check useful information | Existing Market and Holdings, data-source details and refresh states. Existing users retain direct access. |

“Home” is safer than “Today” until content has reliable dates and a real publishing cadence. Treat Bert’s mood as character content, separate from financial status. An illustrative sleepy Bert must not imply a market signal.

## Feature priorities

Effort below is relative engineering and operating complexity, not a delivery estimate. No audience measurements exist to justify a numerical impact score.

| Order | Feature | Smallest useful version | Dependency / cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Home with current BERT activity** | Dispatch, mood and valid event from the existing source, with cached/offline/expired states. | Medium. Define field meanings, freshness and ownership; the source currently lacks post-level metadata. |
| 2 | **Direct experience discovery** | A curated small catalog with explicit destinations, descriptions and working return navigation. | Small–medium. Verify each phone journey and who owns each listing. Favorites are useful only if people repeatedly navigate the catalog. |
| 3 | **Create and personalize** | Bring existing themes/widgets together with Draw Bert. Then add a local caption/sticker compositor using approved BERT assets, exporting through the system share sheet. | Medium for local creation. Native AI generation is a separate integration with quota, session, cost and failure handling. |
| 4 | **Widgets beyond price** | One mascot/dispatch widget or a favorite-experience shortcut, linked to the corresponding app content; retain price widgets. | Medium. Freshness, supported layouts, launcher behavior and artwork QA. Keep it useful as a static glanceable surface. |
| 5 | **A maintained update/event stream** | Dated releases, art drops and occasional community/impact stories, with source links. Optional reminders only after event dates are dependable. | Ongoing editorial cost. Named publisher, archive, expiry and correction workflow are prerequisites. |
| 6 | **Interactive Bert** | Prototype one short “take Bert on a walk” interaction with authored dialogue and a cosmetic keepsake. | Large relative to the other options: writing, animation, state and repeat content. Validate appeal before building a pet simulation. |

The first release should combine priorities 1–3 into a coherent journey: **see what Bert is doing → choose an experience → create or personalize something worth keeping**. This is the proposed value cycle, not an observed user habit. A native caption tool is a useful candidate because it can work offline and provide value beyond a link directory.

Defer a new chat network, wallet login, trading, token-gated access, a full music player and duplicated Woofhub accounts. Each requires a separate user need and ongoing operation. They do not address the current discovery and identity problems. Avoid launching empty “coming soon” destinations as features.

## Practical implementation implications

- Start native with navigation, dispatch/event rendering, cached catalog, themes/widgets and local saved content. Use [Android Custom Tabs](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/android/custom-tabs) for suitable web experiences and app links for Telegram/YouTube. Custom Tabs use browser capabilities and shared browser state; they do not create native authentication or make web generation results automatically available to the app. Provide browser fallback and test returning to the same native state.
- Widget pinning already exists in `StudioScreen.kt:188–194`. Improve discovery and preview quality rather than claiming pinning as a new feature. Android documents [pin requests and launcher support](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/appwidgets/discoverability); a requested pin is not a confirmed placement.
- Introduce a versioned content contract with stable IDs, canonical URLs, `publishedAt`, `updatedAt`, `expiresAt`, availability, and explicit launch types. Prefer a maintained small manifest over HTML scraping. The existing status source can be adapted, but no integration change was made in this pass.
- App branding and character animation should support readable controls. Validate TalkBack labels, font scaling, and touch areas against [Android’s Compose accessibility guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/accessibility/api-defaults), including the 48 dp minimum touch target. Do not infer accessibility from a screenshot or build success.

## What would validate this direction

Recruit a small formative group across existing widget users, BERT community members without holdings, and newcomers. Five to eight people can expose task failures; that sample cannot establish population retention. These are proposed acceptance criteria, not results or industry benchmarks:

1. After a brief look at Home, ask what the app is for. Record whether participants independently identify a non-financial activity.
2. Ask them to find and launch Flappy Bert, find drawing, and set up a widget without help. Record completion, wrong turns, elapsed time and return-to-app behavior. Compare the existing app with the prototype on the same tasks; counterbalance order to reduce learning effects.
3. Ask them to make and share a BERT image. Observe completion of an actual local export/share flow. Opening a share sheet or leaving for a website does not prove that anything was shared or a game was played.
4. Exercise offline startup, stale status, an event past its deadline, unavailable creation, no Telegram/browser handler, large text and interrupted wallpaper application. Record captures and executable checks at the candidate SHA before describing those paths as working.
5. For a subsequent opt-in pilot, define activation as a completed useful action, then measure whether participants return for a non-market activity over seven days. Establish a baseline first. Any telemetry proposal needs an explicit scope; this research added none.

Before implementation, resolve the main unknowns through the content owners: permitted assets, source/API ownership, intended publishing cadence, canonical creation entry point, and which existing community users should test first. Public inspection did not establish backend SLAs, native integration support, artwork reuse terms or actual demand.
