## 1. `0.13.1` Wallpapers & Theme Studio (What Broke or Was Missed)

### 1.1 Orphaned "Ghost Spotlights" on the Empty Home Ledges (`violet-home.html`, `navy-home.html`)
- **What happened:** In `67deaca` / `a8fd2a7`, `<div class="wordmark">` and `<div class="lilac-bar">` / `<div class="amber-bar">` were deleted from `violet-home.html` and `navy-home.html` so the One UI dock wouldn't cover the `BER'T` logo.
- **The bug:** The localized radial spotlight inside `.violet-stage` (`wallpaper-sources-navy-violet/violet-home.html:46`) and `.navy-stage` (`wallpaper-sources-navy-violet/navy-home.html:46`) was left behind:
  - `violet-home.html:46`: `radial-gradient(ellipse 75% 60% at 35% 35%, rgba(153, 46, 255, 0.24) 0%, rgba(124, 0, 255, 0.08) 50%, transparent 80%)`
  - `navy-home.html:46`: `radial-gradient(ellipse 75% 60% at 38% 35%, rgba(255, 148, 51, 0.18) 0%, rgba(255, 148, 51, 0.04) 50%, transparent 75%)`
- **Impact:** That spotlight was positioned at `x = 35%..38%` specifically to backlight the bottom-left wordmark. Without the wordmark, both Home wallpapers (and the live website card `site-violet-card.png`) have an awkward off-center purple/amber smudge on the left side of the empty dock slab illuminating nothing.
- **Fix:** Remove the `radial-gradient(...)` from `.violet-stage` and `.navy-stage` in `violet-home.html` and `navy-home.html` (leaving only the clean linear stage gradient), or center a subtle ambient bounce directly beneath Bert (`at 50% 0%`).

### 1.2 Home Stage Horizon (`y = 2640`) Wastes 560px and Pushes Bert into Row-5 Icons
- **What happened:** The stage ledge on `violet-home.html` and `navy-home.html` was kept at `height: 560px` (`y = 2640..3200`)—which was originally sized to house the 246px `BER'T` wordmark.
- **Impact:** On the S25 Home screenshots (`navy-home-fixed-s.png`, `violet-home-pair.png`), `y = 2640..3200` is now a huge empty slab behind the dock, while Bert (`bottom: 515px`, `y = 2068..2685`) is pushed up directly behind **Row 5 app icons (`Play Store`, `DuckDuckGo`) and their white text labels**, which cut right across his eyes, nose, and glasses, with the page-indicator dots sitting on his chin.
- **Fix:** If the Home wordmark stays removed, consider lowering the Home stage ledge (e.g. `height: 440px`, `y = 2760..3200`) and dropping Bert ~120px lower so his eyes/glasses sit cleanly in the gap between Row 5 labels and the dock, or scaling him slightly so Row 5 labels don't bisect his eyes.

### 1.3 Inconsistency Across the 5 Packs & Website
- **`Mayor Purple` Home still has the dock-clipped wordmark:** `wallpaper_mayor_purple_home.webp` still renders the giant white bottom-left `BER'T` wordmark in the exact dock zone that prompted removing it from `BERT Violet` and `BERT Navy`.
- **No Home preview in `ThemeStudio` (`StudioScreen.kt:228`):** `ThemePackCard` only renders `theme.previewRes` (the Lock screen). Users have a `"Home only"` button (`StudioScreen.kt:299`) but no way to see what the Home wallpaper looks like before applying it.
- **Website `/app/` still says `"one app. four worlds."`:** Even though all 5 packs (`BERT Violet`, `Mayor Purple`, `Woofhub Night`, `Berthalla Nights`, `BERT Navy`) now have full illustrated wallpapers, `/app/` (`theme-grid.png`) still says *"four worlds"* and omits `BERT Navy`.

### 1.4 Hardcoded Violet Colours Inside `ThemeStudio` (`StudioScreen.kt:112-239`)
- **The bug:** `ThemeStudio()` (`StudioScreen.kt:106-202`) and `ThemePackCard()` (`StudioScreen.kt:204-320`) ignore `BERTColors.scheme` and hardcode BERT Violet hex values:
  - `StudioScreen.kt:112-119`: `Color(0xFF120B1B)` background and `Color(0xFF8B37F7)` border
  - `StudioScreen.kt:142`: `Color(0xFFB96DFF)` (`"PHONE THEMES"` label)
  - `StudioScreen.kt:164, 171`: `Color(0xFFCAB5D6)` / `Color(0xFF9E8CAA)` body copy
  - `StudioScreen.kt:211, 239`: `Color(0xFF1A1026)` card background and `Color(0xFF9E8CAA)` subtitle
- **Impact:** When the user taps **Berthalla Navy** in `AppColours()` (`StudioScreen.kt:326`) at the top of `Tools › Style`, the top half of the screen switches to slate-navy (`#0F1722`) with orange accents (`#FF9433`), while `PHONE THEMES` right below it stays neon purple (`#120B1B` / `#8B37F7`).
- **Fix:** Replace the hardcoded violet colors in `ThemeStudio` and `ThemePackCard` with `BERTColors.Panel`, `BERTColors.CardBorder`, `BERTColors.AccentText`, `BERTColors.Cream`, and `BERTColors.Muted`.

## 2. Visual Cohesion & Art Direction

### 2.1 Four Incompatible Visual Styles Stacked on `Home` (`1-today.png`)
Scrolling down `HomeScreen.kt` currently presents four clashing art/type systems in a single viewport:
1. **Unoutlined pastel Godot render + darkly-outlined PixelLab sprite + harsh 2-color Bayer-dithered soil steps** (`BertsPlace.kt:92-185, 419-426`). (Already noted in `NEXT-SESSION.md` line 78 as open art items from round 2, still jarring in `0.13.1`).
2. **Real photographic dog next to pixel-art dog (`HomeScreen.kt:300-314`):** When `MeetBertCard` is visible, `R.drawable.bert_icon` (the circular photo of Bertram in his blue coat) sits `8dp` beside `PixelBertPortrait` (`84dp` pixel sprite).
3. **30sp `Gochi Hand` (`BertHand`) marker text floating over dark fintech/crypto cards (`BertsPlace.kt:228`):** `"inspecting the daisies. they pass."` in 30sp handwriting sits directly above `12sp` `Outfit` sans-serif links (`official: @bertcoincto ↗ bert.global ↗`).
4. **Silent Typography Downgrade in `Berthalla Navy` (`BERTComponents.kt:169`):**
   - `BERTTheme` sets `typography = if (scheme.brandType) Typography().withFamily(Outfit) else Typography()`.
   - Switching to `Berthalla Navy` (`brandType = false`) silently downgrades every `Text` in the app from `Outfit` to system `Roboto`, except where `BertHand` (`Gochi Hand`) is hardcoded. Both schemes should use a deliberate typeface rather than falling back to bare `Roboto`.

## 3. Information Architecture & UX Friction

### 3.1 Berthalla's Daily Dispatch Is Hidden Behind an Unlabelled 34×34 Pixel Post
- **What happened:** In `v0.12+`, Berthalla's daily dispatch (`state.dispatch.note`) was removed from the `Home` scroll feed and moved inside `NoticeboardSheet` (`BertsPlace.kt:440-539`).
- **Impact:** When `fresh` is empty (or after the user has seen the note once), the `"new note on the board"` text link disappears (`BertsPlace.kt:238`), leaving **zero visible text or button on `Home` for the daily dispatch** (`1-today.png`). A user has to know to tap the unlabelled 34×34 art-pixel wooden noticeboard on the right edge of the garden (`SceneHitTargets`, `BertsPlace.kt:396-403`).
- **Broader issue:** All four `SceneHitTargets` (`Chalkboard → Market`, `Gate → Lost Trail`, `Noticeboard → Dispatches`, `Long-press Bert → Meet Bert`) are invisible `Box` overlays with no visual affordance.
- **Fix:** Always keep a subtle, persistent affordance under `BertVoiceLine` (e.g. a small `"Read the noticeboard ›"` chip even when `fresh` is empty, switching to `"N new on the board"` when fresh) so the daily writing is never hidden behind a mystery meat pixel tap.

### 3.2 `Home` and `Play` Are 60% Duplicate Screens (`HomeScreen.kt:103-120` vs `PlayScreen.kt:55-78`)
- **The issue:** `Home` renders `"through the gate"` + `LostTrailContinueCard` + `TournamentCard` (`1-today.png`). Tapping `Play` in the bottom nav (`4-play.png`) renders the exact same `"through the gate"` + `LostTrailContinueCard` + `TournamentCard` at the top of the screen, followed by `BertMusicCard` and two static `"Coming Next / On The Drawing Board"` placeholder cards.
- **Fix:** Either make `Home`'s game entry a compact single-row status/continue strip so `Play` owns the full hero cards + leaderboard + arcade catalog, or differentiate what appears on `Home` vs `Play`.

### 3.3 `Create` Tab Pushes Every Control Below the Fold (`CreateScreen.kt:101-147`, `2-create.png`)
- **The issue:** In `CreateScreen.kt`, `CaptionPreview` (`CreateScreen.kt:298-411`) renders at full 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratio inside the scrolling `Column`. On standard 9:16 phones (see Play Store screenshot `2-create.png`), the preview consumes the entire screen above the fold: the `Your caption` `OutlinedTextField` is cut in half by the bottom nav bar, and `ArtPicker`, `Layout`, `Size`, `Colours`, `Share`, and `Save` are completely off-screen.
- **Impact:** Users typing a caption or toggling `Layout`/`Size`/`Colours` below the fold cannot see `CaptionPreview` updating without scrolling up and down after every tap.
- **Fix:** Either constrain `CaptionPreview`'s max height / pin a compact preview above a scrollable control sheet, or place `ArtPicker` + `Your caption` immediately visible above the fold.

### 3.4 Confusing 3-Way Theme Split in `Tools › Style` (`CreateScreen.kt:52-70`)
- **The issue:** `PersonaliseScreen` (`Tools › Style`) stacks three separate theme/colour sections on one screen:
  1. `APP COLOURS`: `BERT Violet` vs `Berthalla Navy` (changes in-app UI colours only).
  2. `MY BERT`: 6 poses / NFT lookup (changes Home chip, widget picture, card art).
  3. `PHONE THEMES`: 5 packs including **`BERT Violet`** and **`BERT Navy`**, each with `"Use palette on widgets"` and `"Apply both / Home only / Lock screen only"`.
- **Impact:** Tapping `Berthalla Navy` in `APP COLOURS` does not change the widget palette or wallpaper to `BERT Navy`, and tapping `BERT Navy` in `PHONE THEMES` does not change `APP COLOURS`. Having `BERT Violet` and `BERT Navy` appear in two separate pickers on the same screen controlling disconnected state is confusing.
- **Fix:** Either unify `APP COLOURS` with `PHONE THEMES` (or add a `"Use for app, widgets & wallpaper"` / `"Match app colours"` toggle) or clarify the labels so the same pack names aren't duplicated across two unlinked pickers.

## 4. Code & Engineering Debt

### 4.1 Dead `TodayGarden` Composable + Dead `today_garden.png` Asset (`TodayGarden.kt:45-120`)
- **The issue:** Since `v0.12` replaced the `600×136` garden banner with `BertsPlace`, `TodayGarden(...)` (`TodayGarden.kt:45-120`) and `R.drawable.today_garden` (`res/drawable-nodpi/today_garden.png`) are never called anywhere in `src/main`—only in legacy tests (`TodayGardenGroundTest`). `BertsPlace.kt` and `HomeScreen.kt` only use the helper functions at the bottom of `TodayGarden.kt` (`rememberReducedMotion`, `forever`, `pixelBertSize`, `PixelBertPortrait`, `moodStill`).
- **Fix:** Extract the live helpers into `BertSprites.kt` (or `BertsPlace.kt`), delete the dead `TodayGarden(...)` composable and `today_garden.png`, and update/retire `TodayGardenGroundTest` in favor of `BertsPlaceTest`.

### 4.2 No Lifecycle-Safe State Holder in `MainActivity.kt` (`BERTScreen`, `MainActivity.kt:86-173`)
- **The issue:** Inside `BERTScreen`, all repositories (`BERTStateRepository`, `BERTPriceHistoryRepository`, `BERTMarketHistoryRepository`, `BerthallaActivityRepository`) and all UI state (`tab`, `toolsSection`, `state`, `history`, `marketHistory`, `activityState`) live in plain `remember { ... }` blocks (not `rememberSaveable` or a `ViewModel`).
- **Impact:** Any configuration change (rotation, split-screen resize, foldable posture change, system font-scale/theme toggle) destroys the composition, resets `tab` back to `Home`, re-instantiates every repository, and restarts all 3 network polling loops from scratch.
- **Note from `NEXT-SESSION.md` (line 56):** The previous `PROPOSAL-2026-10-23` ViewModel draft was rightly rejected because it didn't compile and dropped `refreshMarketHistory()`. A proper fix should either:
  - Minimum low-risk fix: use `rememberSaveable` for `tab` and `toolsSection` so config changes don't kick the user back to `Home`, OR
  - Full fix: move the 4 repositories and all 3 loops (`refresh()`, `refreshActivity()`, `refreshMarketHistory()`) into an `AndroidViewModel` covered by unit tests.

### 4.3 15-Second Root Recomposition Tick (`MainActivity.kt:153-172`)
- **The issue:** `BERTScreen` updates `var now by remember { mutableStateOf(System.currentTimeMillis()) }` every `15_000L` ms at the root of composition and passes `now` into `BERTPriceHistory.combine(marketHistory, history, now)` and `BERTApp(...)`.
- **Impact:** Every 15 seconds, the root composable recomposes and re-merges price arrays even when the user is on `Create`, `Play`, or `Tools › Style`.
- **Fix:** Scope the relative-time ticker to the composables that actually display `"as of Xm ago"` (or only recompute `combinedHistory` when `marketHistory` or `history` changes, since 5-minute GeckoTerminal bars don't change on a 15-second local clock tick unless pruning expired points).
