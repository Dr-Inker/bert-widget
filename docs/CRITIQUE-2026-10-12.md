# BERT 0.9.0 critique — 2026-10-12

A max-effort critique, made read-only, from host renders, the Play shots, captures from the S25 and the v090 source.
This is the work list for 0.9.1 and 0.10.

## Verdict

0.9.0 is not premium yet. It is a careful, honest and accessible Material 3 app, but next to Finch, Duolingo,
Nintendo Today! or Pokémon Sleep it reads as a well-built crypto dashboard with a dog photo pinned on.

The single biggest gap is that **Bert is not alive**. The app has:

- no animation (no androidx.compose.animation), no haptics and no custom font;
- one photo stamped on every surface;
- every screen built as a stack of navy cards under an orange caps label.

## Top problems (ranked)

1. **Bert never moves and nothing responds to touch.** Every state change is a hard cut. The cached update swaps
   visibly for the fresh one. The card preview blanks to "Updating preview…" on every edit (CreateScreen.kt:65-69).
   Fix:
   - Put the game's owner-approved pixel Bert in the Today card, using the 48px sheets in
     `/opt/bert-platformer/assets/expedition/` (bertIdle, bertBark, bertJump, bertWalk), drawn nearest-neighbour.
     He idles with a small bob, barks with a haptic when tapped, and jumps as the pull-to-refresh indicator.
   - Animate the update text in (fade plus an 8dp rise).
   - Animate the price: digits slide up or down with the move.
   - Crossfade between tabs.
   - Keep the previous card preview until the new one is rendered, then crossfade (150ms).
   - Haptics: toggle on and off for switches, confirm on save and share, a segment tick on tabs.
2. **Home has no hierarchy.** It is seven near-equal cards, each with a different 1dp border colour. The static
   Official BERT card sits third, above the game. The hero has three actions, and "Refresh" wraps onto its own
   indented row. Fix:
   - Order: Today, price strip, Lost Trail, tournament, checklist, journal.
   - Lost Trail becomes full-width 16:9 art with "Continue · <world>" and a 6-segment progress bar.
   - The Official links become two small chips under the wordmark. The full card stays on Explore only, once.
   - Replace Refresh with pull-to-refresh (PullToRefreshBox).
   - One hero button, "Make it a card". Berthalla on X becomes a small icon link.
3. **No typographic or visual identity.** There are 18 font sizes, 12 corner radii, three header styles, and
   palettes that disagree (app navy, black-purple default widget, purple Personalize, lilac cards).
   - 0.10: Fraunces for Bert's voice, with a 6-step type scale.
   - 0.9.1: three radii (12/20/28) and no coloured borders; make the navy and orange default the widget theme.
4. **The Today card can't tell you what's new.** "From Berthalla · 13:55" is the feed's regeneration time, not the
   post time. The greeting has only four strings. The mood is a price-derived pill styled like a status chip.
   Bug: narrow Bert widgets cut the mood to 9 letters, so "MELANCHOLY" shows as "MELANCHOL"
   (BERTDailyWidget.kt:118, :130). Fix:
   - Use the journal's first-seen time, with a "New" marker, then "first seen 09:12".
   - Remove the truncation.
   - Set the mood as a phrase in Bert's voice, not a status chip.
5. **One photo of Bert, and no introduction.** Fix:
   - A first-run "Meet Bert" card: a real Pomeranian called Bertram, the cartoon is drawn from him, and Berthalla is
     the fan project.
   - Owner photos, rotated by date (0.10).
   - Remove the photo from the Create and Tools headers.
6. **The widgets sell themselves badly.** Fix:
   - The picker previews are loading layouts. Use the host renders as `previewImage`, and show the same images above
     each Add button in Personalize.
   - Add a time at 2x2 and a 10sp minimum.
   - Glance 1.2 generated previews later.
7. **Create is a form, and Share is the weaker button.** Fix:
   - A 72dp thumbnail row of the five artworks.
   - Make Share the filled primary button.
   - Put a small BERT wordmark and "berthalla.io/app" at the foot of exported cards.
   - Offer Telegram and X first in the share sheet.
8. **The store listing undersells the app and leaks names.** Fix:
   - The fixtures use real players' handles (PlayStoreScreenshotsTest.kt:47). Use fictional names and a
     Monday-morning clock.
   - Order: gameplay, widget, Today, card maker (photo art), Market.
   - Add a short caption band on each shot.
   - The icon has a blurred halo; use a full-bleed icon.
9. **Nothing rewards coming back.** Fix:
   - "Day N with Bert": it counts up, never resets and is never pushed.
   - A "2 new from Bert" chip.
   - A tournament results card when an event ends.
10. **The copy reads like a privacy notice.** Fix:
    - Move the mechanics into one (i) row per screen.
    - UK spelling throughout ("Personalise").
    - Curly apostrophes everywhere, plus a test that fails on a straight apostrophe in `Text("…")`.
    - Remove the duplicate loading line (BERTComponents.kt:33).

Also:

- Fade scrolling content under the pinned tab header.
- At large text, the bottom nav becomes a 2x2 grid taking about 22% of the screen, and the date stacks onto three
  lines (use "MON 12 OCT").
- "· 25 sells" can start a line with a dangling separator.
- There is no launcher shortcut to continue the game.

## Looks AI-made or templated

- Navy, one orange accent and cream text on rounded cards.
- An orange caps label on almost every card.
- Typed characters as icons (↗ ✓ ○ ›), where the app has its own drawn icon set (BERTIcon.kt).
- Per-card coloured borders.
- A three-beat tagline on every screen.
- The middle dot as the separator in nearly every line.
- The market widget's accent stripe and stat tiles.
- The Moonlight (Berthalla Nights) art.
- `/opt/uploads/Bert_multi_shade.png` carries a Gemini watermark. Never ship it.
- Over-reassurance everywhere.
